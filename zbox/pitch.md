# AviCV

**AviCV is a CV that rewrites itself for every job, and shows you a receipt for the time it gave back.**

*Your CV, already in flight.* · avicv.com

## What it is

Most CV tools are fancy text editors. AviCV is a career agent with a memory. You tell it your story once: every role, project and win goes into a single master profile. From then on, you never write a CV again. You point it at a job, and it builds the right one.

## How it works

1. **You talk, it listens.** A chat agent sits inside the app and has your whole master profile in reach.
2. **It goes hunting.** Through Darwin, a search engine for AI agents, it finds specialist agents for job postings, salary data or company research. It recruits help the way you'd recruit a team.
3. **It tailors.** For each job, it picks and rewrites the bullets that matter and saves the result as a variant, so your original is never touched.
4. **It keeps score.** Every task logs how long it took against how long it takes by hand, so the time-saved counter is evidence, not marketing.

Under the hood, the agent can only touch your data through four narrow tools. It's powerful, but it can't wander.

## Problems and their answers

| The problem | AviCV's answer |
| --- | --- |
| Tailoring a CV to one job eats most of an hour | The agent does it in seconds from your master profile |
| "CV_final_v7_REAL.pdf" | One profile, unlimited saved variants, fix a typo once |
| Job hunting means twenty browser tabs | The agent finds and briefs other agents for you |
| AI tools claim savings nobody can check | Every automation leaves a timed receipt |
| Agents with access to your data are unnerving | Four tools, one job each, nothing else reachable |
| Your CV is stale the day you need it | The profile grows as you work, so you're always ready |

## Best in the world at

**Turning one career into the right CV for any job, and proving the minutes it saved.**

Other tools help you write a CV. AviCV is the first one that treats your career as data, your job search as a team sport between agents, and your time as something worth measuring out loud.

## The bigger idea

A CV is just the first document. The same loop of one profile, many agents and a receipt for every minute works for cover letters, grant applications, visa forms and anything else where people retype their own life story for a stranger.

## Why "Avi"

"Avi" is the Latin root for bird, as in aviation. The name carries the speed.
